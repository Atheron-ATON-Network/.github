# Security policy

Email **security@aton-network.org**. Do not open a public issue.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, which repository and commit, and your read on the impact. Acknowledgement within 72 hours, status updates at least weekly until it is closed.

Several components are scheduled for independent third-party audit before mainnet: the tensor-memory-hard mining lane, the cross-VM router, and every contract that moves value. Internal review is not treated as a substitute for those audits and neither is a good report from you, but a good report will absolutely change what the auditors are pointed at.

A standing bounty program will be announced before mainnet. Reports made before then remain eligible for retroactive recognition and payment.
